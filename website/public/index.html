[07]FAQ

The honest answers.

Does NOVA Workspace actually open .docx files without breaking them?

Yes. NOVA Write reads and writes .docx — ECMA-376, the open ISO standard Microsoft also uses — plus .rtf, .odt and .txt. The engines were built clean-room from the public specification; nothing in the suite was copied from or disassembled out of Microsoft Office.

What exactly happens after the 30 days?

You choose. Keep NOVA Workspace running for ₹6 a month, and nothing changes about the software — every feature, every export, every application. Or stop, and the suite simply stops unlocking: your files are ordinary .docx, .xlsx, .pptx and .pdf documents on your disk, always openable elsewhere. Nothing is held hostage, and no upgrade screen blocks your work.

Is this really ₹6? What's the catch?

₹6 a month, for one person, for the whole suite — and that is the entire price list. We do not sell seats, tiers, fonts, “Pro” buttons, support contracts or an enterprise plan to upsell you into. The cap is our bandwidth, not your wallet.

Do I need a card or an account to start the trial?

Neither. Download the zip, unzip it, launch NOVA Desk — the 30-day trial has already started. We do not even ask for your email, because we have nowhere to send one. When the trial ends, if you want to keep going, the app will ask for ₹6 and offer a note-to-pay route — no portal, no invoice download, no “contact your administrator”.

Why charge anything at all?

Because downloads and servers cost money, and because "free forever" is usually funded by the price of your data or the hope of an acquisition. ₹6 a month is honest: it pays for the bandwidth that serves you this 89 MB download, and nothing else. If ₹6 is not possible for you this month, write to us and we waive it. No proof, no form, no judgement.

Do you collect anything about me?

No. NOVA Workspace has zero telemetry, no crash reporter, no analytics ping, no “help us improve” checkbox pre-ticked. The only network traffic is the one you create by opening a link — or by the apps refusing to phone anywhere, ever, because they simply cannot.

Can I cancel Microsoft 365 today and switch entirely?

For .docx, .xlsx, .pptx and .pdf work, yes — today. NOVA Write covers 505 of Word’s cataloged features; NOVA Grid covers 276 of Excel’s 292; NOVA Deck covers 84% of PowerPoint’s breadth; NOVA PDF covers 452 Acrobat features. Install the suite, point it at your files, and cancel whenever you’re comfortable. Nothing in NOVA Workspace requires an export step or a migration assistant.

What about Microsoft Teams / OneDrive / SharePoint collaboration?

NOVA Workspace is deliberately an authoring suite, not a walled garden. Your files live where you put them — a network share, a USB stick, or your own sync of choice. If your organisation collaborates inside Microsoft Teams, keep your files in Teams and edit them with NOVA: the apps never see your network. We are not building a surveillance suite; we are fixing the part that writes your documents.

Who builds it, and can I trust them?

A small team that got tired of paying for a word processor. NOVA Workspace is written and maintained by us; the source stays in our own private repository, and we would rather be judged on what the software does than on a licence. The engineers were built clean-room from the published OOXML and PDF specifications, so nothing in the suite was taken from anyone else's code. Try it for 30 days, then decide if ₹6 a month is worth it to you.

Can NOVA Write type Tamil (Bamini)?

Yes. NOVA Write includes Bamini Tamil input, on by default, so typing on any keyboard produces Tamil the way Bamini typists learned. The Madhuram (TAU-Marutham) typefaces and other Tamil fonts are bundled with the suite. It handles front vowel signs, ஸ்ரீ, ர/ொ/ோ/ௌ and ள combinations, Tamil quotation marks, and Sanskrit/vada mozhi letters as you type. Toggle Tamil mode from the status-bar chip (தமிழ் பாமினி / EN).

Does it run on Windows 7, 8.1, 10 and 11?

Yes — Windows 7 SP1, Windows 8.1, Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). The four applications are single, self-contained executables built for x86_64-win7-windows-msvc, so the same download runs across those machines. NOVA Desk (the home screen) is a .NET Framework 4.8 application: that runtime is built into Windows 10 (version 1903 and later) and Windows 11, and on Windows 7 SP1 and 8.1 it is a free install through Windows Update. Without it the launcher explains what’s needed and the four applications still start directly from the folder.

macOS and Linux: the engines build there, but this published download is Windows-only.
